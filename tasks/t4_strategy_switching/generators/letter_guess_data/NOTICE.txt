This wordlist is derived from /usr/share/dict/words on macOS (BSD heritage),
which is itself derived from Webster's 2nd International Dictionary (1934).

The list of English words is a list of facts and is not copyrightable
(Feist Publications v. Rural Telephone Service Co., 499 U.S. 340 (1991)).

This file contains all 5-letter lowercase-alphabetic English words from
that source, extracted via:

    awk 'length==5 && /^[a-z]+$/' /usr/share/dict/words

No attribution is required; included here as a stable, reproducible word
universe for the letter-guess strategy-switching task.
